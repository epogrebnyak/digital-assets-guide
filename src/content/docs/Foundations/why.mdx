## What is blockchain?

Append-only, distributed ledger for data storage and program execution secured by cryptography and consensus of trustless participants.

## Why build it?

Several core motivations underpin blockchain development, at least in the original intent:

- censorship resistance – no central authority can block or reverse transactions,
- self-custody – users retain control over their assets,
- universal access – anyone with an internet connection can participate,
- transparency and privacy.

> See ["Decoding Bitcoin: A Cypherpunk's Manifesto for Financial Freedom"](https://d-central.tech/decoding-bitcoin-a-cypherpunks-manifesto-for-financial-freedom/).

Yet during waves of wild speculation, these same technologies can get exploited and attract scams.

## Milestones

- 2008–2015 – [Bitcoin](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf) (2008) and [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/whitepaper) (2015) launches.
- 2013–2014 – First crypto boom (BTC hits $1,000).
- 2017–2018 – [ICO](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/credit-suisse-argues-irrational-exuberance-around-icos-indicates-bitcoin-bubble-2018-01-15) boom.
- 2020–2021 – [DeFi summer](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/with-comp-below-$100-a-look-back-at-the-defi-summer-it-sparked-2020-10-20) + [NFT](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10320355) explosion, [L2 scaling](https://blockworks.co/news/das-2021-are-layer-1s-better-than-layer-2s) (2021).
- 2022 – Crypto winter: [Terra/Luna](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/konferenser/2023/session-1-liu_makarov_schoar-anatomy_of_a_run-_the_terra_luna_crash.pdf) collapse, 3AC, [FTX](https://www.chicagofed.org/publications/chicago-fed-letter/2023/479).
- 2023–2024 – Institutional adoption sarts with ETFs.
- 2025–2026 – [Peak BTC](https://www.htx.com.gt/price/btc/history/?start=1738368000000&end=1769904000000&period=1mon&pageNo=1), [MiCA](https://oraclelawglobal.com/news/stablecoins-in-the-european-legal-order-regulation-systemic-risk-and-the-transformation-of-financial-infrastructure-2025-update/) and [GENIUS Act](https://dcbar.org/news-events/publications/washington-lawyer-articles/genius-act-a-regulatory-first-step,-not-the-final-) for stablecoin regulation, AI agents, RWAs, regulatory clarity emerging.
- 2026 – [Tokenization and institutional adoption](https://www.dtcc.com/dtcc-connection/articles/2026/may/19/tokenization-moves-from-theory-to-reality).
